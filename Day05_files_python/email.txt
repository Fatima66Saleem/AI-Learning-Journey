Subject: My Amazing Trip Across Pakistan

Hi,

I hope you're doing well! I just wanted to share some highlights from my recent trip across Pakistan. It has honestly been one of the most amazing experiences of my life.

I started in Hunza Valley, where I saw breathtaking mountain views and visited the old Baltit Fort. From there, I traveled to Skardu and spent a day exploring Deosai National Park and the stunning Shangrila Resort.

After the north, I went to Lahore, where I visited the Badshahi Mosque and the Lahore Fort. I also spent an evening at the food street in Old Lahore, trying so many delicious local dishes.

My favorite part of the trip was Naran and Kaghan Valley, where I saw Lake Saif-ul-Malook surrounded by snow-capped mountains. The water was so clear and the whole place felt like a dream.

I ended the trip in Multan, exploring the old shrines and buying some beautiful handmade pottery to bring home.

I still can't believe how much I got to see and do in just a few weeks. I have so many photos and stories to share with you when we meet next.

Talk soon,
Fatima
